I'm Johnson Samuel, training as a DevOps engineer in Nigeria.

I work through fifteen role-play projects. Each is a fictional company with its own codebase, language and infrastructure problem — invented scenarios, chosen to force a specific set of decisions.

The companies are made up. The infrastructure is not. I build on real AWS, break it deliberately, and write up what actually happened, including the theory I chased first and why it was wrong.

This is the record, kept as I go.
